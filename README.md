# Novel Prompter

## What is Novel Prompter?
Greetings, Novel Prompter is an application that assists writers who want to write full length works of fiction (or even non fiction) with the help of AI. More specifically, it is an open source alternative to Novel Crafter.
## So this app has AI?
No. It doesn't connect to LLMs directly (yet, as of release 1.0). Unlike Novel Crafter, which requires you to connect to OpenRouter (or similar services) to generate words, this app only provides a framework for you to instead use any chatbot of your choice, such as ChatGPT or Perplexity. So, you're basically copy pasting things from the app into the chatbot and vice versa as you work on your book instead of working exclusively in this app. 
## If it doesn't use AI, then what does it actually do?
Novel Prompter aims to be an open source alternative to Novel Crafter. So, it basically tries to do what Novel Crafter does behind the scenes but gives you full control. It mimics the prompts that Novel Crafter sends to LLMs and presents them to you so you can copy-paste them into the chatbot of your choice. So, it is helping you write in a more organized way, like Novel Crafter, but without some of its conveniences (which, may I remind you, Novel Crafter locks behind a monthly fee + whatever extra money you pay for the actual words generated)
## If it doesn't have AI, then it is of no use to me
Fair enough. I made this because I already had a monthly subscription to a chatbot and didn't want to pay separately for generating words.
## Don't listen to that guy! I'm on board. Tell me how to use it.
Ok, if you have used Novel Crafter, you already have the gist of how to use Novel Prompter. Add your characters, locations, and things to the entries section. Then add a chapter and then add a scene to that chapter. In the scene summary, you basically write what you want to happen in the scene. Then, you click on the Generate Prompt button in the Text section. Novel Prompter will then create a prompt that includes the System Prompt, your current scene summary, and the summaries of any scenes that came before it. So, basically, in every prompt it generates, you have everything you need to get the text of the scene from your chatbot. Paste that long prompt Novel Prompter generates into the chatbot and you should get the text for the scene you're writing. Novel Crafter does all of this too, but it does it in the background.
## What is the difference between the Idea Prompt and Generate Prompt button?
The Idea Prompt button generates a prompt that when entered into a chatbot will give you ideas for what the next scene should be and the different directions the story could go in. It is not a replacement for Generate Prompt and I suspect most people won't use it at all.
## I don't know. All of this sounds very complicated.
It can be intimidating at first. But Novel Prompter presents you with the prompt. So I urge you to read it to understand what the app is doing. It just lays out all the information logically for the LLM to help it spit out the next scene. That's it! Trust me. Just read the prompts that it generates and it will click for you.
